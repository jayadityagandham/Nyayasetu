Petitioner
Maneka Gandhi
Respondent
Union of India
Facts
The petitioner's passport was impounded by the Regional Passport Authority under the Passport Act, 1967, without assigning any reasons therefor. The petitioner challenged the impoundment on the ground that it violated her fundamental right to personal liberty under Article 21.
Issue
Whether the right to travel abroad is a part of the right to personal liberty under Article 21, and whether the procedure prescribed by law must satisfy requirements of fairness and reasonableness as required by Articles 14 and 19.
Ratio
The Supreme Court held that "procedure established by law" in Article 21 must be just, fair and reasonable. No person can be deprived of life or personal liberty except in accordance with a procedure that is reasonable, fair and just. Articles 14, 19 and 21 are not mutually exclusive.
Decision
The Court ruled in favour of the petitioner, holding that the impoundment of the passport without any opportunity of being heard was violative of the principles of natural justice and therefore unconstitutional. The order was set aside.
